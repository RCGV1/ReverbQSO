# ReverbQSO Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

ReverbQSO is an independently developed amateur-radio client for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. It connects to the EchoLink service and stations you choose. It is not operated by Synergenics, LLC, the provider of EchoLink. This policy describes ReverbQSO's current behavior, including its companion apps.

Privacy contact: **benjaminfaer@gmail.com**. Please do not send passwords, license documents, or recordings to this address unless they are necessary for a request and you have agreed to share them.

## Information used to operate the app

| Information | Purpose and destination |
| --- | --- |
| Callsign and EchoLink password | Sign in and maintain your EchoLink directory presence. These are sent to EchoLink's authentication/directory service. Callsigns also identify you to connected stations and selected relay or proxy servers. |
| Email, callsign, chosen password, and an installation-specific software identifier | Sent to EchoLink when you explicitly submit its account-registration form. The identifier is generated by the app and is not an advertising identifier. Registration and license validation are administered by EchoLink. |
| Microphone audio, transmitted tones, and text messages | Sent to the station or conference you connect to. Other participants can hear/read them. A relay or proxy you use handles the connection traffic. |
| IP address, connection metadata, and station availability | Used by EchoLink, stations, and relay/proxy operators to route and maintain connections. A network service can observe the address from which you contact it. |
| Station directory and participant information | Retrieved to browse stations, apply filters, and show who is in a QSO. Country/region labels are inferred from callsigns and station information; they are not a measurement of your device's location. |
| Optional support or deletion requests | The information you choose to send goes to the recipient shown in the app or form. Requests about an EchoLink account go to EchoLink support, not the ReverbQSO maintainer. |

The app does not require a separate ReverbQSO cloud account. The maintainer does not operate a server that receives your normal QSO audio, messages, or passwords. ReverbQSO has no advertising, advertising identifier collection, behavioral tracking, or automatic analytics/crash-report upload to the maintainer.

## Information stored on your devices

- Saved EchoLink and custom-proxy passwords are stored using Apple's Keychain. Callsigns, preferences, favorites, recent connections, connection restrictions, and cached directory/proxy information are stored locally. Saved logins remain until you remove them; caches may be replaced as the app refreshes them.
- Chat and live connection state are used to display the current session. The app does not provide a cloud chat archive.
- Recording is off by default. If you start a clip or enable Always Record, received and transmitted audio is saved locally as WAV files with contact/time metadata. Recordings remain until you delete them. Exported copies are controlled by the destination you choose, such as Files, a cloud-drive provider, or another app.
- The paired iPhone can send saved login information, favorites, recent stations, QSO state, and optional forwarded receive audio to Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. The Watch saves its login in its own Keychain so it can operate independently. Remove saved logins separately on devices where they were stored; removing one device's copy does not revoke another device's copy.
- The Mac menu-bar companion exchanges local QSO status and control commands with the Mac app. This local exchange is not sent to a ReverbQSO server.
- Apple's system logs can contain diagnostic information such as callsigns, station/proxy addresses, route information, and connection errors. Debug/test builds can produce additional local diagnostics when explicitly enabled. There is no automatic upload to the maintainer. Review diagnostic files before sharing them.

Device backups, Keychain persistence, system logs, and exported files are also governed by your Apple/device settings and the relevant provider. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily remove its Keychain items or copies in backups.

## Permissions and system features

Microphone access is used for transmission. Local-network access is used when connecting to a station or proxy on your local network. Notification permission, where offered, enables local call/message notifications. Live Activities show current QSO status on system surfaces. You can control permissions in system settings. ReverbQSO does not request your device's GPS location, contacts, photo library, or advertising-tracking permission.

Declining microphone access permits receive-only use. Notification previews, Live Activities, and the Mac menu bar can reveal callsigns or message/session information to someone who can see your device. Use the app's and system's notification/lock-screen controls to limit that visibility.

## EchoLink, stations, proxies, and external links

EchoLink is a separate service with its own [privacy policy](https://secure.echolink.org/privacypolicy.htm). Its policy describes its handling of account and login information. Stations and conference participants may record, retain, or redistribute what you transmit. Public and custom proxy/relay operators can observe traffic they carry. ReverbQSO cannot delete those parties' copies or control their retention practices.

EchoLink radio traffic is not end-to-end encrypted. Do not treat a QSO, text message, or public proxy as a private or confidential channel. HTTPS is used for supported web operations such as registration and CQ, but that does not encrypt the entire radio session or every legacy EchoLink directory operation.

Opening this policy on GitHub, EchoLink validation/support pages, or another external link contacts that site's operator and is subject to its policy. License validation documents are handled by EchoLink's validation process; they are not uploaded to the ReverbQSO maintainer. Apple handles App Store distribution and any diagnostics you separately choose to share with Apple under its own terms.

## Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect a QSO, sign out, remove a saved login, remove favorites/history entries, delete recordings, and change recording/notification/audio preferences in the app. Removing a saved login removes that device's saved credentials and ends its account session; it does not delete your EchoLink registration or unrelated recordings/favorites.

To request deletion of your EchoLink account and its service-held data, use **Request EchoLink Account Deletion** in the app and complete the official EchoLink support process. EchoLink handles that request and any identity checks or retention obligations. Opening a request form is not confirmation that an account has been deleted. Local copies on the paired Watch or another device and exported recordings must be removed separately.

For information you voluntarily send to the ReverbQSO maintainer, contact **benjaminfaer@gmail.com** to request access, correction, or deletion. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related follow-up or applicable obligations. The maintainer cannot retrieve or delete information held only by EchoLink, another station, a proxy operator, Apple, or your chosen export provider.

## Changes
