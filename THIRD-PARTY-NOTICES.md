## Callsign allocation data

Country classification uses the [ITU Table of International Call Sign Series, Appendix 42](https://www.itu.int/gladapp/Allocation/CallSigns), reviewed on 2 October 2026. These allocations identify a callsign country, not a station’s coordinates or current physical location.
